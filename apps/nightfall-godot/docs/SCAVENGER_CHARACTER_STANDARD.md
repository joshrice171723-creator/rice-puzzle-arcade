# Scavenger Character Standard

> **Direction update (2026-10-21):** This document remains a reference for Scavenger identity and legacy class art. Its older rule requiring gameplay to preserve the adult class-page silhouette is superseded. New playable characters follow [GAMEPLAY_CHARACTER_ART_PLAN.md](GAMEPLAY_CHARACTER_ART_PLAN.md): the original chunky, illustrated gameplay style, separate from class-page portrait proportions.

The adult blue-coat Scavenger is Nightfall's canonical character authority.
Every Scavenger presentation uses this identity: an adult face, a lean athletic
silhouette, long lower-body proportions, dark tousled hair, a cream scarf,
blue-and-gold coat, layered brown leather, fitted dark trousers, tall boots,
and a compact brass firearm.

## Three production assets

1. **Class showcase** — the high-detail canonical cover. It may add breathing,
   a weight shift, blinking, cloth sway, and weapon glow, but it may not swap
   in a different face or body.
2. **Gear paper doll** — derived layers on the same full-size canvas. Armor
   adds color and material overlays to the adult silhouette; it never replaces
   the actor with a shorter or more cartoonish body.
3. **Gameplay actor** — the same layered adult source at combat scale. The
   head, core, arms, cape, and legs are separate draw layers, with joint-based
   walking, recoil, dodge lean, hit flash, and a restrained idle.

The old chibi Scavenger atlas remains only as a historical source until all
four classes receive their adult production rigs. It must not be selected for
the Scavenger in the Gear page, Base, Class page, or Expedition.

## Anatomy guardrails

- Figure height reads as at least six and a half head heights.
- Hip-to-floor distance is visibly greater than chest-to-hip distance.
- Boots end at the foot; no stretched shin or duplicated foot is permitted.
- The held weapon anchors to the right wrist; the wrist, elbow, and shoulder
  maintain plausible joint lengths through an attack or dodge.
- Menu motion is subtle: a 1–2 px breathing/weight shift at menu scale.

## Equipment presentation

Body, pants, boots, and helmet each receive an authored mask from the adult
source. Set color accents communicate the equipped family while preserving the
underlying silhouette. Ring, charm, and gadget anchors are positioned on the
same rig and remain readable at Gear-page size.
