## Grid Foundry

- Title: Grid Foundry
- Short description: Fit shapes, clear lines, and make room for your next clever move.
- Proposed identifier: `com.joshuarice.gridfoundry` (verify availability before permanent registration).

Make every square count. Place shapes on an 8 by 8 board, fill rows and columns, and clear space for the next hand. Plan around three pieces at a time and turn a tight board into a satisfying combination.

Play at your own pace, chase a local personal best, or try the day's seeded challenge. Clear tile outlines help you read the board. No player account is required, and ordinary play works offline.

An optional rewarded video can offer one second chance per run: clear the two busiest rows and receive a playable hand. Video availability varies. A fresh game is always available. Occasional full-screen ads may appear between eligible completed runs; ads require connectivity and applicable privacy permission.

## Merge Drift

- Title: Merge Drift
- Short description: Swipe matching numbers together and discover your next milestone.
- Proposed identifier: `com.joshuarice.mergedrift` (verify availability).

A board of numbers. Four directions. Room for one more move. Swipe to slide tiles, combine equal numbers, and build larger values on a compact 4 by 4 board. Each merge adds to your score, while each successful swipe brings a new tile into play.

Keep a path open, discover new milestones, and challenge your local best. Try a daily seeded board or start a new run whenever you like. Ordinary play works offline without a player account.

At the end of a run, an optional rewarded video can remove the two smallest tiles for one rescue. A new run never requires a video. Occasional full-screen ads may appear between eligible completed runs. Videos depend on connectivity, availability and applicable privacy permission.

## Color Cascade

- Title: Color Cascade
- Short description: Tap matching symbol groups and chase your best in 90 seconds.
- Proposed identifier: `com.joshuarice.colorcascade` (verify availability).

Find a group. Tap to clear. Watch the cascade. Match three or more identical symbols connected along their sides on a 7 by 7 board. Bigger groups earn bigger scores as new symbols fall into place.

Each ordinary round gives you 90 seconds of foreground play. Pause for a break and return when you are ready. If there are no matches, shuffle for free. Both color and shape distinguish the symbols.

Build a local personal best and try the daily seeded challenge. No player account is required; ordinary rounds work offline. After time runs out, an optional rewarded video can add 20 seconds once per round. Replay remains available without a video. Occasional full-screen ads appear only between eligible completed runs, subject to availability and privacy permissions.
