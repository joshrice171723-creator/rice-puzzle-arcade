  • Describe the steps that led to the problem.

  • Include a screenshot only if it contains no personal information.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No player account is required. Progress is local to your device.

Why is an optional video unavailable?

Video availability depends on connectivity, privacy settings and the advertising service. You can always start another ordinary round.

Where can I download the games?

The Android games are in development. No public store release is announced here yet.

How do I delete my progress?

Clear the app's storage through Android settings or uninstall the app. This removes local progress. See the privacy policy for advertising-service data.
