Color Cascade

Find your flow in ninety seconds.

Spot connected groups of matching symbols. Tap to clear, watch the board tumble, and chase your personal best before time runs out.

Android edition in development. Store release has not been announced.

◆●●▲◆◆●■✦▲▲■✦✦▲■

Board illustration · Android game in development

A few simple rules.

  1. Tap three or more matching symbols connected along their sides.
  2. Bigger groups earn bigger scores as new symbols fall into place.
  3. Play a ninety-second round. If no groups remain, shuffle for free.

Progress and personal bests stay on your device. Ordinary play is designed to work offline. Optional ads require a network connection and applicable privacy permissions.
