Merge Drift

Small moves. Bigger possibilities.

Slide a four-by-four board of numbers. Bring equal tiles together and discover just how far a thoughtful swipe can take you.

Android edition in development. Store release has not been announced.

24481616326464128

Board illustration · Android game in development

A few simple rules.

  1. Swipe to slide every tile in one direction.
  2. Matching tiles combine once per move into a larger number.
  3. A new tile joins after each successful move. Keep a path open.

Progress and personal bests stay on your device. Ordinary play is designed to work offline. Optional ads require a network connection and applicable privacy permissions.
