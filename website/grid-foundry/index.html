Grid Foundry

Make room for your next good move.

Fit shapes onto an eight-by-eight board. Complete rows and columns, build combinations, and keep the foundry moving.

Android edition in development. Store release has not been announced.

■■■■■■■■■■

Board illustration · Android game in development

A few simple rules.

  1. Choose one of three shapes and place it in an empty space.
  2. Fill rows and columns to clear them together.
  3. Plan ahead: the run ends when none of your shapes fit.

Progress and personal bests stay on your device. Ordinary play is designed to work offline. Optional ads require a network connection and applicable privacy permissions.
