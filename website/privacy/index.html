Privacy, clearly.

This policy covers Rice Puzzle Arcade, Grid Foundry, Merge Drift, and Color Cascade. The Android apps are in development. Production advertising and store releases have not been verified. This policy describes the app design and included advertising tools; it must be checked against each final release.

Your game progress

Scores, saved runs, tutorials and preferences are stored on your device. No Rice Puzzle Arcade player account or game server is required. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes this local data; your operating system may independently provide backup features.

Advertising and Google

The apps integrate Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Development builds use test advertising. When advertising is permitted, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, app interactions, diagnostic data, and device or account identifiers, including an advertising identifier where available. Google uses these for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit.

Google's handling and retention follow its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. Local game-data deletion does not delete information held independently by Google.

Your choices

Where required, an advertising privacy message appears before eligible ad requests. Use the in-app Privacy choices control to revisit available choices. Android also provides advertising identifier controls in system settings. Refusing optional permissions does not prevent ordinary gameplay. Ads require an internet connection; optional video benefits are granted only after the advertising service confirms a reward.

Analytics and crash tools

Optional gameplay analytics sends a random installation identifier, session identifier, game and level activity, scores, results, play duration and ad outcomes to our studio at aimetaclinic.com. Production installations default to analytics off; enable or disable it in Settings, Privacy and support. Internal test builds enable test analytics by default. No name, email, location or advertising identifier is included in studio events. Raw events expire after 90 days and unsent events after seven days. Turning analytics off clears unsent events and rotates the installation identifier. Previously received records expire on the retention schedule. Hosting infrastructure processes network requests; the collector does not store IP addresses. Google advertising measurement is separate.

Children and release audience

The owner has not yet finalized the intended age audience or Google Play audience declarations. Public distribution is pending that decision and any resulting child-directed advertising safeguards. This development policy makes no claim of Families certification or child-directed release approval.

This website and support

The site does not add advertising, tracking scripts or a contact form. If hosted on GitHub Pages, hosting requests are handled by GitHub under its privacy statement. Support issues on GitHub are public. Please omit personal details and identifiers. GitHub may require an account to submit an issue.

Retention, deletion and questions

Local progress remains until it is replaced, cleared or the app is removed. For support content you submit through GitHub, use GitHub's edit and deletion controls. For Google's data, use Google's privacy controls. For policy questions, contact the project through support; do not post private information. We will update this page when app data practices change.
